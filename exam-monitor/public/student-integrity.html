Activity monitoring and academic integrity

To support fair assessment and academic standards, Examio records your responses, submission status, and activity within the assessment. Depending on your instructor’s settings, this may include leaving the test page, loss of window focus, fullscreen changes, and attempts to copy or paste.

I agree to complete my work honestly and independently, follow my instructor’s assessment rules, and use AI or other assistance only when expressly permitted. I understand that suspected misconduct may be reviewed under the applicable course and university policies.

For Sejong University courses, AI use must follow the syllabus and instructor’s instructions. Unauthorized AI use in tests or assignments may constitute academic misconduct.

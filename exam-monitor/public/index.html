HOW IT WORKS

Run a test in three steps

  1. Launch a test

    Choose a saved quiz. Use Quick Launch with your saved preferences.

  2. Let students join

    Project the QR code. Students join in their browser. Start together and follow their progress live.

  3. Review and export results

    Review answers and scores. Download Excel or PDF, email results, or save to Google Drive.

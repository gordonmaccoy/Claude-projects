HOW IT WORKS

Run a test in three steps

  1. Launch a test

    Choose a test from your library and open the classroom display on your projector.

  2. Let students join

    Students scan the QR code and join in their browser. Start when everyone is ready and follow their progress from your private dashboard.

  3. Review and export results

    See scores as students submit and download your class results as an Excel file.

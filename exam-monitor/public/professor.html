Ask Examio

AI help uses the Examio guide. It cannot access your tests or student records, or make changes. Your typed conversation and the guide are sent to OpenAI. Answers may be mistaken. Do not share student records, passwords, answer keys, or secrets. This temporary conversation clears when you sign out.

Replies use your verified email. Omit student records and secrets. If delivery is unavailable, your request is queued for support.

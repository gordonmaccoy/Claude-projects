Using Examio

Examio provides browser-based tests, calculator sessions, page-visibility monitoring, grading, and results exports. Professors are responsible for their account credentials and for the tests, answer keys, settings, and grading decisions they use. Use material you have permission to upload and process.

Professors must inform students about the information collected, the public classroom display, and the monitoring and scoring rules before a session begins. Professors must arrange any institutional approvals or permissions required for their use.

Assessments and AI

Page visibility and connection events can be affected by device behavior or interruptions. Professors should review relevant events and circumstances when making assessment decisions. A monitoring event alone does not establish why a student left the page.

AI drafts and proposed answer keys may contain errors. Review questions, answer keys, and point values before using them in an assessment. Help chatbot responses are guidance and may also be mistaken.

Content and acceptable use

You retain your rights in content you provide. You permit Examio and its service providers to process that content to deliver the features you use. Students' class records must be used and shared only as appropriate for the assessment and applicable institutional requirements.

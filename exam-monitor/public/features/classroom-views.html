PRIVATE CONTROL. SHARED CLARITY.

One class. Three connected views.

Keep your controls on your computer, put the class display on a projector, and let students answer on their own phones or tablets. Everything runs in the browser.

Get started

The right information on the right screen.

▤

Professor: your private control center

Launch and start tests, set options, pause or extend time, review student progress, and see detailed answers. Student numbers and grades stay in your private dashboard.

▣

Classroom: project the big picture

Show the room name, website address and expandable QR code. Students see connection and submission status. Student numbers and individual scores are not shown publicly.

▯

Students: join and answer

Scan the QR or enter the room name on examio.io, then provide a student number and name. Answer in the browser with no app installation or student account.

From joining to submitting.

  1. Launch and project.

    Choose a saved quiz, open the class display, and move that window to your projector. Keep the professor dashboard on your own screen.

  2. Let students join.

    Use a custom room name or a short, silly generated name. Students enter their details, then wait until you start.

  3. Start together.

    The timer begins when you start the test. Students use the interface language you selected, while your quiz content stays as written.

  4. Finish with clear feedback.

    Choose whether students see their final score and corrections. After submission, their attempt stops collecting inactivity events.

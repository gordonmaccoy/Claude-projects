KEEP YOUR CLASSROOM IN VIEW

Real-time monitoring & cheating deterrents

See student activity as it happens. Choose the deterrents that suit your class, and stay in control when an interruption needs a second look.

Get started

Know what is happening, while it happens.

●

Activity at a glance

Active students appear green. Hidden pages or focus changes can appear red when the relevant monitoring option is enabled. Submitted attempts have their own clear status.

Ⅱ

Pause without penalties

Pause the test to lock student screens and freeze the countdown. Inactivity monitoring pauses too, then resumes with fresh grace time.

↻

A professor makes the final call

Review monitoring events privately. Unlock a failed attempt to continue, or finalize a failed submission at zero while retaining the original score.

Set the rules before the test.

One master switch

Turn monitoring and deterrents off together. Students can still join, answer and submit, and appear green on the class display.

Five-second page-hidden rule

When enabled, a test page hidden for more than five seconds triggers FAIL and locks the attempt. A lost network connection alone does not trigger this rule.

Focus and fullscreen events

Record focus changes and fullscreen transitions. These events are logged for review; they do not independently trigger automatic FAIL.

Make copying less convenient

Choose text-selection restrictions and copy/paste blocking. Shuffle questions and answers separately for each student. Correct answers stay matched to each question.

Browser-based, with clear limits.

Fullscreen is a request that students accept with a tap. Examio cannot block screenshots or lock a student’s device. These tools discourage distraction and provide activity signals for the professor to review.

FROM YOUR MATERIAL TO CLASS RESULTS

Create the quiz. Let grading keep up.

Build questions manually, import a quiz you already have, or ask AI to prepare a draft. Then follow answers in real time and export the results.

Get started

Start with what you already have.

Create manually

Write multiple-choice or fill-in-the-blank questions. Add images, rearrange questions, and set point values for more important items.

↑

Upload or paste a quiz

Upload a PDF or Word DOCX, or paste existing questions. Import supplied answer keys directly, or use AI to organize the questions and propose missing answers.

✦

Generate with AI

Describe a topic, provide source material, or combine both. Choose question types and a question count, then review the editable draft before saving.

Results that are ready to use.

Automatic, weighted grading

Grade saved answer choices and configured accepted text answers automatically. Point values feed the total score and percentage.

Live detailed results

See correct and incorrect responses in a green/red grid. Review class completion, average scores and question-level performance.

Feedback you control

Show final scores and corrections after submission, or keep them hidden. Failed attempts retain their original score for professor review.

Export and organize

Download summary or detailed grades as Excel or PDF, or email Excel to your signed-in address. Keep results named by class or section.

AI use stays deliberate.

AI preparation sends the material you approve to the configured AI provider. API usage charges may apply. Always check question accuracy and answer keys before assigning a quiz.
